How it works

Four steps, each one on record.

01

List your skills

Pick what you know and what you want to learn. The matcher counts the skills you share with every mentor and ranks them for you.

02

Send a request

A focus area and a short pitch. The database turns away duplicates, full mentors and anyone who is not a student.

03

Talk once accepted

Chat opens only for an accepted pair. The mentor decides, and a mentor cannot accept more people than they set.

04

Meet, then review

Book a time shown in your own timezone. After the meeting is marked complete, the mentee rates it once.
